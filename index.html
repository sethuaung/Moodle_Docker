About LMS

Moodle is a powerful Learning Management System (LMS) designed to provide educators, administrators, and learners with a single robust, secure, and integrated system to create personalized learning environments.

Moodle Features

Course Management

Organize, track, and deliver courses with ease.

Assessments & Quizzes

Create interactive quizzes, assignments, and feedback tools.

Collaboration Tools

Forums, chats, and wikis to enhance learning interaction.

Analytics & Reports

Track learner progress and performance with detailed insights.

Usage

Moodle is used by schools, universities, companies, and organizations worldwide to deliver online courses, blended learning, and training programs. Its flexibility makes it suitable for small classes to large-scale deployments.

Worldwide Community

Moodle has a vibrant global community of developers, educators, and learners. Thousands of plugins, forums, and collaborative projects make Moodle a truly community-driven platform.

Learning Academy

Moodle Academy provides free learning resources, webinars, and training courses to help educators and administrators maximize their use of Moodle.
